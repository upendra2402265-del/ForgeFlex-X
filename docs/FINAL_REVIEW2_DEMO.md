# ForgeFlex X — Final Review 2 Demo

## 1. What the reviewer sees

ForgeFlex X is presented as a production-aware industrial energy command center:

```text
Forecast → FlexDNA → MILP → 24-hour schedule → Digital twin → Simulink
```

The UI is intentionally organized around a decision cycle rather than a collection of charts.

## 2. Three-minute demonstration

### A. Start

Run:

```powershell
python -m streamlit run app.py
```

Open the local Streamlit page.

### B. Normal Day

Keep:

- Scenario: Normal Day
- PV: 700 kW
- Battery: 500 kWh
- Battery power: 250 kW
- Grid limit: 900 kW
- Objective: Balanced

Press **EXECUTE 24-HOUR OPTIMIZATION**.

Say:

> “ForgeFlex forecasts the renewable and industrial demand profile, then solves a constrained MILP to determine how grid, storage and flexible processes should be scheduled.”

### C. Cloudy Day

Select **Cloudy Day** and execute again.

Say:

> “The renewable condition changed. The optimizer therefore recomputes the energy allocation instead of replaying a fixed schedule.”

### D. High Production

Select **High Production** and execute.

Say:

> “The industrial demand increased, so the energy requirement changed while the production constraints remain hard constraints.”

### E. Grid Constrained

Select **Grid Constrained** and reduce the grid limit.

Say:

> “Now the external supply constraint changes. The MILP must find a schedule that respects the lower grid limit, or explicitly report that no feasible schedule exists.”

### F. FlexDNA

Open the FlexDNA tab.

Point to the machine timeline:

- Air Compressor — 150 kW, 2 h, window 09:00–16:00
- Chiller — 110 kW, 3 h, window 10:00–17:00
- Water Pump — 75 kW, 2 h, window 08:00–18:00

Say:

> “This is our main re-engineering idea: process flexibility itself becomes an energy resource. We move required operating blocks within their legal production windows instead of simply switching the factory off.”

### G. Digital Twin

Open the Digital Twin tab.

Show:

- Battery SOC trajectory
- Energy-balance residual
- Validation matrix
- Grid headroom

Say:

> “Python is the decision engine. The digital twin independently checks the resulting energy trajectory before engineering handoff.”

### H. Simulink

Open the Simulink model and show the Power Flow Scope followed by the Energy Balance Scope.

Say:

> “The Simulink layer is the engineering validation environment for the operating setpoints generated by the optimizer.”

## 3. What not to claim

- Do not claim physical machine switching is implemented.
- Do not call the Streamlit animation itself the MILP solver.
- Do not present synthetic-dataset savings as measured factory savings.
- Do not imply a real SCADA/PLC integration exists today.

## 4. Strong closing line

> “We optimize energy around production, not production around energy.”
